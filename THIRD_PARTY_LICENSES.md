third-party open-source software. This file summarizes the direct components;
the authoritative list is `package-lock.json` and `src-tauri/Cargo.lock`.

This repository intentionally does **not** include Gerber, BOM, CPL, STEP,
or other fabrication outputs. The tested board and its source files are not
published here. Any manufacturing files generated by users are their own
responsibility and may be subject to additional licenses.

## Notices for binary distributions

and the Node.js license text that accompanies the embedded sidecar binary.
Do not remove copyright or license notices from bundled assets.
